# Family and operations workflows

Updated September 29, 2026.

- Parents: Members → Manage your family on web; More → Family on mobile. Edit contact name/email/phone/address, add players, and edit player names/ages. Contact email does not change sign-in credentials. New players await staff team placement. Parents cannot alter membership, family ownership, teams or jersey numbers.
- Waitlists: Programs on web and mobile. Join full or queued programs, view your own position and withdraw. Staff offer spots in persisted FIFO order. Offers reserve capacity for 48 hours; accepting requires normal registration, current form answers and waiver acknowledgement. Declining or expiry releases capacity. Expired entries can rejoin at the end. Offered/registered players cannot join twice; concurrent offers cannot oversubscribe capacity. Staff manually issue the next offer; there is no automatic promotion or payment charge.
- Training: Manage plans and assignments on web; More → Training on mobile. Edit name, notes and exercise order, archive/restore reusable plans, and cancel unfinished exercises for an assignment session. Future assignments use edited plans; existing snapshots and completed results remain intact. Cancelled workouts cannot be completed and no longer generate reminders. Native plan creation and weekly roster assignment are now implemented; see COMPLETION-BATCH.md.
- Mobile navigation: Home, Schedule, Chat and More provide access to all screens. New forms have explicit labels and 48px minimum controls; primary app buttons are larger. This is a usability improvement, not a completed screen-reader/hardware accessibility audit.
- Reports: Operations → Admin reports on web; More → Reports for staff/admin on mobile. Enrollment, attendance, unpaid invoices and inventory are searchable and refreshable. Web supports filtered CSV exports with spreadsheet-formula escaping. Attendance includes historical recorded players and distinguishes not-recorded from absent. Payment totals retain currency separation. Mobile shows the first 100 matching rows and directs larger exports to web.
